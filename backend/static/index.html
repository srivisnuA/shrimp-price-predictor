How the forecast is built

The model uses lagged regional price, temperature, rainfall, disease-event pressure and shrimp aquaculture production. Future weather/disease/production values can be supplied as scenarios; otherwise the model uses historical seasonal values.

Forecasts are statistical estimates, not trading or market advice.
