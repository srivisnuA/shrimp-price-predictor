How the forecast is built

Both price series are kept separately. Weather, disease outbreaks, aquaculture production, market/macro variables and lagged prices drive the forecast. The farm-gate target only uses months where a real farm-gate observation exists; missing farm-gate months are not silently replaced by export prices.

Forecasts are statistical estimates, not trading or market advice.
